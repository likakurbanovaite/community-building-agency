About Us

Community as a resilient way to grow.

Community Building Agency was created for fashion and creative businesses navigating an uncertain commercial environment. As visibility becomes harder to gain and sustain, customer retention becomes more challenging, and paid reach less predictable, community offers a more resilient way to grow. We help brands build community as a strategic growth system that strengthens loyalty, deepens customer relationships, sharpens positioning, supports long-term sales and creates social, emotional, cultural and commercial value.

What We Do

We design Community Ecosystems for fashion and creative businesses.

Strategic framework

  • Defines community purpose, shared identity, values and intended audience.

  • Designs participation, touchpoints, rituals and member experiences.

  • Creates pathways through which relevant people discover and enter the community.

  • Supports continued participation, recognition and return over time.

  • Considers how value is created, exchanged and contributed across the community.

  • Creates opportunities for relationships and exchange between members.

  • Identifies indicators and metrics for tracking community development.

We use this framework to develop community strategies tailored to each client’s goals, challenges and opportunities.
