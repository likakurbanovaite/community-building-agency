About Our Community

CBA is a growing community for people connected to fashion, creativity and business.

We are here for the ones who do not want to create and grow alone. Good ideas need people to make them real. They need conversation, trust, encouragement, shared resources and someone saying “I can help with that.” We are building a space where people can find collaborators, ask for help, offer support, create opportunities and feel more connected and excited in their creative journeys.

We welcome different backgrounds, cultures, disciplines and perspectives, because meaningful creative work is rarely built from one point of view. This community is for people who care about co-creation, generosity, cultural exchange, social impact and collective growth.

What we stand for

Our Community Culture & Values

Generosity & Mutual Support

We believe in helping each other when we can, sharing knowledge, time, ideas and encouragement without always expecting something in return.

Understanding & Open-Mindedness

We welcome different backgrounds, cultures and perspectives. We encourage curiosity, respectful conversations and a genuine willingness to understand each other.

Collectivity & Initiative

We believe in the power of collective effort. Everyone has something valuable to contribute, and we encourage people to bring forward their own ideas, take action and support each other's initiatives. By getting involved and working together, we can achieve more and make the journey easier and more joyful for everyone.

Making the World a Better Place

We believe that even small acts of kindness, support and understanding can make a difference. By helping each other, caring about others and taking action when we can, we hope to contribute to a more compassionate, connected and better world for humans.

How do we put our values into practice

The Way We Treat Each Other

We encourage:

  • ✓ Asking for help and offering support when possible
  • ✓ Sharing ideas, knowledge, opportunities and useful resources
  • ✓ Sharing your work, projects and achievements
  • ✓ Taking initiative and contributing to the Community
  • ✓ Being open to different perspectives and experiences
  • ✓ Respecting people's time, boundaries and creative work
  • ✓ Giving credit to others for their ideas and contributions

We don't accept:

  • ✕ Discrimination, harassment, bullying or disrespect
  • ✕ Shaming or discouraging members from asking for voluntary help
  • ✕ Pressuring others to help after they've declined
  • ✕ Spam, repetitive advertising or unsolicited sales messages
  • ✕ Taking credit for someone else's work or sharing it without permission
  • ✕ Violating members' privacy or personal boundaries

A little reminder: Asking for help is always welcome, whether you have a budget or are simply hoping someone might be willing to lend a hand. Offering help is voluntary, and everyone is free to decide what they can contribute. We believe generosity comes in many forms, and even the smallest act of support can make a difference.

CBA Community Guidelines & Terms of Participation

By joining the CBA Community, you agree to the following.

1. Who this is for

The CBA Community is open to people connected to fashion, creativity and business, as well as anyone who shares our values and wants to connect, help others and make a positive difference.

Membership is at CBA's discretion, and we may decline or remove access for anyone who does not align with the Community's values or breaches these guidelines.

2. How we use your information

When you join, we collect the details you share in the sign-up form, including your name, email address, Instagram handle, phone number, optional WhatsApp number, location, background, what you need help with, how you could help others and how you heard about CBA.

We use this information to:

  • Process your registration and give you access to the Community and its channels, including the Instagram Community Chat.
  • Contact you about your membership, Community activities and relevant opportunities.
  • Understand who is in the Community so we can facilitate introductions, identify opportunities for collaboration and plan activities based on members' interests and needs.
  • Respond to your questions, feedback, requests to correct your information or reports of inappropriate behaviour.

We use your Instagram handle to add you to our main Community Chat. Providing a WhatsApp number does not automatically enrol you in our optional WhatsApp group.

We will only send you promotional emails if you have separately agreed to receive them. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may use third-party services to manage registrations, store information and communicate with members, subject to appropriate data protection arrangements.

We keep your information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, including any applicable legal obligations.

You can request access to your personal information, ask us to correct inaccurate details or request deletion by contacting us. Other rights under applicable data protection law may also apply.

For further information about how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information, please see our Privacy Policy.

3. Instagram, WhatsApp and other Community channels

Our main Community Chat operates on Instagram. We also offer an optional WhatsApp group for additional discussions and sharing resources.

These platforms have their own terms and privacy policies, which are outside CBA's control.

Please be mindful of what you share in Community chats. Messages, profile information and other details may be visible to fellow members, depending on the platform and its privacy settings.

Community conversations are not moderated in real time. If you notice inappropriate behaviour or content that breaches these guidelines, please report it to CBA.

4. How we expect members to behave

This Community works because people treat it with care. By joining, you agree to:

  • Respect other members, their work, time and boundaries.
  • Share ideas, knowledge, opportunities, useful resources and your own work.
  • Be open to different perspectives, backgrounds and experiences.
  • Contribute to the Community and support others when you can.
  • Respect other members' decisions about whether and how they can help.
  • Credit other people's ideas, work and contributions properly.
  • Respect members' privacy and obtain permission before sharing their personal information or work.

The following behaviour is not accepted:

  • Discrimination, harassment, bullying, threats or disrespectful behaviour.
  • Shaming or discouraging members from asking for voluntary help.
  • Pressuring members to provide help, services or resources after they have declined.
  • Spam, repetitive advertising or unsolicited sales messages.
  • Taking credit for someone else's work or sharing it without permission.
  • Sharing members' private information or communications without their permission.
  • Violating members' privacy or personal boundaries.

Members are welcome to ask for help, including voluntary support with projects or opportunities for which they may not have a budget. Offering help is always voluntary, and members are free to decide what they can contribute.

Members may share their work, projects, services and achievements when relevant to the Community. Promotional activity must remain respectful of other members and these guidelines.

CBA may remove content, restrict participation or remove members who breach these guidelines. We may take immediate action without prior notice when reasonably necessary to protect members or the Community.

5. Events and in-person activity

If you attend CBA events, pop-ups or activities, you are responsible for taking reasonable care of your own safety and belongings.

CBA is not responsible for loss, damage or injury arising from your attendance, except where caused by our negligence or where liability cannot legally be excluded or limited.

6. No guarantee of outcomes

CBA provides a space for connection, collaboration and opportunity, but we do not guarantee any specific business outcome, collaboration or result from your participation.

Any agreements, projects or working relationships you form with other members are between you and them. CBA is not a party to these arrangements unless expressly agreed otherwise and is not responsible for their outcomes, except where liability cannot legally be excluded or limited.

7. Changes to these terms

We may update these guidelines from time to time as the Community grows. If we make significant changes, we'll let members know through the Community's usual communication channels.

8. Leaving the Community

You can leave the Community at any time by leaving the Instagram Community Chat, leaving the optional WhatsApp group or contacting us directly.

Leaving a group chat does not automatically delete the information you provided when registering.

If you would like to end your membership and request deletion of your personal information, please contact us using the email address below. We will process your request in accordance with applicable data protection law.

You can unsubscribe from promotional emails separately at any time.

9. Contact

If you have questions about these terms, how your information is used, your membership, or wish to report inappropriate behaviour, contact us at communitybuildingagency@gmail.com.

We welcome feedback and encourage members to contact us with any concerns.
