About Our Community

CBA is a growing community for people connected to fashion, creativity and business. We are here for the ones who do not want to create and grow alone.

Good ideas need people to make them real. They need conversation, trust, encouragement, shared resources and someone saying “I can help with that.” We are building a space where people can find collaborators, ask for help, offer support, create opportunities and feel more connected and excited in their creative journeys.

We welcome different backgrounds, cultures, disciplines and perspectives, because meaningful creative work is rarely built from one point of view. This community is for people who care about co-creation, generosity, cultural exchange, social impact and collective growth.

CBA Community Guidelines & Terms of Participation

By joining the CBA Community, you agree to the following.

1. Who this is for

The CBA Community is open to people connected to fashion, creativity and business who want to contribute to a space built on collaboration, not just self-promotion. Membership is at CBA's discretion, and we may decline or remove access for anyone who does not align with the community's values.

2. How we use your information

When you join, we collect the details you share in the sign-up form (name, email, Instagram handle, location, age range, and your interests). We use this information to:

  • Give you access to the community and its channels (email, WhatsApp)
  • Contact you about updates, events and opportunities
  • Understand who is in the community so we can make better introductions and plan better events

We do not sell your information to third parties. You can ask to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it removed at any time by contacting us.

By submitting the form, you agree to CBA storing your responses and contacting you by email or WhatsApp about community updates, events and opportunities. You can unsubscribe or leave the community at any time.

3. WhatsApp and other community channels

Some community activity happens on WhatsApp or other third-party platforms. These platforms have their own terms and privacy policies, which are outside CBA's control. Please be mindful of what you share there, as messages may be visible to other members and are not moderated in real time.

4. How we expect members to behave

This community works because people treat it with care. By joining, you agree to:

  • Respect other members, their work, time and boundaries
  • Share in a way that adds value to the community
  • Be open to connection, collaboration and mutual support
  • Offer help, advice, introductions or feedback when you genuinely can
  • Credit other people's ideas, work and contributions properly

And to avoid:

  • Spam, repeated self-promotion, or posting only to sell services
  • Harmful, discriminatory, offensive or exploitative content
  • Contacting members privately for sales, promotion or requests without context and respect
  • Sharing another member's personal information, contact details or work without permission

CBA may remove content or members that go against these guidelines, without notice, if we believe it's needed to protect the space.

5. Events and in-person activity

If you attend CBA events, pop-ups or activities, you do so at your own risk. CBA is not responsible for loss, damage or injury arising from your attendance, except where this is caused by our negligence.

6. No guarantee of outcomes

CBA provides a space for connection, collaboration and opportunity, but we don't guarantee any specific business outcome, collaboration, or result from your participation. Any agreements, projects or working relationships you form with other members are between you and them, and CBA is not a party to them and holds no responsibility for their outcome.

7. Changes to these terms

We may update these guidelines from time to time as the community grows. If we make significant changes, we'll let members know through the community's usual channels.

8. Leaving the community

You can leave at any time by unsubscribing, leaving the WhatsApp group, or contacting us directly to have your information removed.

9. Contact

If you have questions about these terms or how your information is used, contact us at communitybuildingagency@gmail.com.
