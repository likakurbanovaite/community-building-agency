Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Community Building Agency ("CBA", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, request a consultation, join the CBA Community, participate in community activities or engage with our services.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

2. Who We Are

Depending on how you interact with Community Building Agency, we may collect the following information.

Consultation requests and business enquiries

  • Full name
  • Email address and telephone number
  • Business, brand or project name
  • Website or social media profile
  • Professional background and business information
  • Business goals, challenges and community-building activities
  • Consultation availability and scheduling preferences
  • How you heard about CBA
  • Information provided through correspondence or consultations
  • Communication and marketing preferences

CBA Community membership

  • Full name and email address
  • Instagram handle, where provided
  • City and country
  • Selected age range
  • Professional role or background
  • Areas of interest, such as events, networking, learning and collaborations

  • Membership and communication preferences

Events and community activities

If we collect additional information for a particular event, we will explain its purpose when requesting it.

Website and technical information

Depending on the technologies used on our website, we may collect technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device information, website usage data, referral sources and cookie preferences.

We encourage you to avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific purpose that has been discussed with you.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries and consultation requests
  • Arranging and conducting introductory consultations
  • Understanding business objectives and preparing service proposals
  • Delivering agreed consultancy and implementation services
  • Reviewing and managing CBA Community membership
  • Welcoming members and providing access to community activities
  • Communicating relevant community information, events and opportunities
  • Facilitating introductions, connections and collaborations where appropriate
  • Planning events, resources and community experiences
  • Maintaining a respectful and safe community environment
  • Managing professional relationships and business administration
  • Sending optional marketing communications where legally permitted
  • Maintaining website functionality and security
  • Understanding website usage and improving our services
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

We use personal information only where an appropriate lawful basis applies.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases, depending on the purpose and circumstances.

Legitimate interests

We may process personal information where reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, administer community membership, organise activities, manage professional relationships, maintain security and develop our services.

We consider the potential impact on individuals and their rights when relying on legitimate interests.

Contractual necessity

We may process personal information where necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract with you or to fulfil an existing contract with you.

Consent

Where required, we rely on your consent for optional marketing communications and certain cookies or tracking technologies.

You may withdraw consent at any time.

Legal obligations

We may process and retain personal information where necessary to comply with applicable legal, accounting, tax or regulatory requirements.

6. Consultation Requests and Business Enquiries

When you submit a consultation request through our website, we use the information provided to review your enquiry, contact you, arrange an introductory consultation and prepare for our discussion.

We may also use this information to understand your objectives, assess whether our services are suitable and identify potential next steps.

Submitting a consultation request does not automatically subscribe you to optional marketing communications.

7. CBA Community Membership

When you apply to join the CBA Community, we use the information provided in your registration form to review your application, manage membership and understand your professional background and interests.

We may use this information to:

  • Welcome you into the community
  • Provide information about community activities and communication channels
  • Share relevant community events, resources and opportunities
  • Facilitate introductions and connections where appropriate
  • Understand members' interests and improve community experiences
  • Manage participation and uphold the Community Guidelines & Terms of Participation

We may contact members by email with information directly related to their membership, community activities and relevant opportunities.

Optional promotional marketing communications are managed separately, where required by law.

Community participation is subject to the CBA Community Guidelines & Terms of Participation.

You may leave the community at any time by contacting us or leaving the relevant communication channels.

8. WhatsApp and Other Community Platforms

Some CBA Community activities may take place through WhatsApp or other third-party communication platforms.

Participation in these channels is optional.

Depending on the platform and its settings, other members may be able to see your telephone number, profile information, messages and other information you choose to share.

These platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms of service.

We encourage members to review the privacy settings of any third-party platforms they use and to consider carefully what personal information they share.

We cannot guarantee the deletion of messages or information already viewed, copied or retained by other participants.

9. Events and Community Activities

We may process personal information to organise and administer CBA events, meetups, workshops and other community activities.

This may include managing registrations, communicating event information, recording attendance and collecting feedback.

Where we intend to photograph, film or otherwise record an event, we will provide appropriate information about the recording and its intended use.

Additional permissions or privacy information will be provided where required.

10. Marketing Communications

You may choose to receive occasional news, insights, updates and promotional communications from Community Building Agency.

Where consent is required, we will request it separately through an optional opt-in.

You can withdraw your consent or unsubscribe at any time by following the instructions in our communications or contacting us directly.

We may retain limited information necessary to record and respect your communication preferences.

Unsubscribing from optional marketing does not prevent us from sending essential communications relating to an enquiry, service or active community membership.

11. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential functionality, maintain security, remember preferences and understand how visitors interact with the website.

Certain technologies are necessary for the website to function. Others may be used for analytics or marketing purposes.

Where required by applicable law, we will request your consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

You can manage applicable cookie preferences through the controls provided on our website and through your browser settings.

Information about the cookies and technologies used, including their purposes and applicable retention periods, will be provided through the website's cookie information and consent controls.

12. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share personal information with trusted third-party service providers where necessary to operate our website, manage community activities, communicate with members or deliver our services.

These may include providers of:

  • Website hosting and form processing
  • Email and communication services
  • Community messaging platforms
  • Video conferencing and scheduling
  • Event registration and administration
  • Cloud storage and business management
  • Website analytics and security
  • Professional advisory services

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, we seek to ensure that appropriate data protection arrangements are in place.

We may share limited information with other community members where necessary for an agreed introduction or collaborative activity, taking account of your preferences and reasonable expectations.

We may also disclose personal information where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

13. Client and Community Data Processed Through Our Services

As part of our consultancy and implementation services, Community Building Agency may be given access to personal information relating to a client's customers, community members, event participants or other individuals.

Where we process this information solely on behalf of a client and under its documented instructions, the client will generally act as the data controller and Community Building Agency will act as a data processor.

Such processing will be governed by appropriate contractual arrangements, including data protection terms required by applicable law.

Where Community Building Agency independently determines the purposes and means of processing personal information, we will assess our responsibilities as a data controller and provide any additional privacy information required.

Individuals whose information is processed through a client's community activities should also refer to the relevant client's Privacy Policy.

14. International Data Transfers

Some third-party service providers may process or store personal information outside the United Kingdom.

Where international transfers occur, we will take steps to ensure they comply with applicable UK data protection requirements, including the use of appropriate safeguards where necessary.

You may contact us for further information about relevant international transfers and safeguards.

15. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

Our retention arrangements include:

  • Consultation enquiries that do not lead to a client relationship: Up to 12 months after the last meaningful communication, unless a longer period is justified.
  • CBA Community membership information: For the duration of membership and for up to 12 months after membership ends, unless a longer period is necessary for a specific legal or legitimate purpose.
  • Event registrations and participation records: For the period reasonably necessary for event administration, follow-up and applicable legal requirements.
  • Client records and correspondence: For the duration of the client relationship and any additional period required for legal, contractual or legitimate business purposes.
  • Marketing information: Until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, subject to periodic review. Limited suppression records may be retained to respect your preferences.
  • Website analytics and technical information: In accordance with the applicable retention settings of the services used.
  • Personal information processed on behalf of clients: In accordance with the relevant client agreement and documented instructions, subject to applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymise it.

16. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration and disclosure.

These measures may include access restrictions, account security controls, secure storage and the use of reputable service providers.

Access to personal information is limited to those who require it for legitimate business purposes.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

17. Your Data Protection Rights
