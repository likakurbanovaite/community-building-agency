1. Introduction

Community Building Agency ("CBA", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, request a consultation, join the CBA Community, participate in community activities or engage with our services.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

We use personal information for the following purposes:

We use personal information only where an appropriate lawful basis applies.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases, depending on the purpose and circumstances.

Legitimate interests

We may process personal information where reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, administer Community membership, facilitate relevant introductions and opportunities, organise activities, manage professional relationships, maintain security and develop our services.

We consider the potential impact on individuals and their rights when relying on legitimate interests.

Contractual necessity

We may process personal information where necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract with you or to fulfil an existing contract with you.

Consent

Where required, we rely on your consent for optional marketing communications and certain cookies or tracking technologies.

You may withdraw consent at any time.

Legal obligations

We may process and retain personal information where necessary to comply with applicable legal, accounting, tax or regulatory requirements.

The lawful basis applicable to particular information depends on the purpose for which it is used. You may contact us for further information about the lawful basis for a specific processing activity.

6. Consultation Requests and Business Enquiries

When you submit a consultation request through our website, we use the information provided to review your enquiry, contact you, arrange an introductory consultation and prepare for our discussion.

We may also use this information to understand your objectives, assess whether our services are suitable and identify potential next steps.

We may retain relevant correspondence and consultation notes for business administration, follow-up and maintaining relevant professional relationships, subject to the retention arrangements described in this policy.

Submitting a consultation request does not automatically subscribe you to optional marketing communications.

7. CBA Community Membership

When you apply to join the CBA Community, we use the information provided in your registration form to review your application, manage membership and understand your background, interests, needs and potential contributions.

We may use this information to:

  • Welcome you into the Community
  • Add you to our main Instagram Community Chat using the Instagram handle provided
  • Provide information about Community activities and communication channels
  • Share relevant Community events, resources and opportunities
  • Facilitate introductions and connections where appropriate
  • Understand members' interests and improve Community experiences
  • Manage participation and uphold the Community Guidelines & Terms of Participation

We collect telephone numbers as an additional way to contact members about their membership, relevant Community opportunities, introductions and collaborations. We may use these details where appropriate to facilitate connections or follow up on Community-related matters.

Providing a WhatsApp number does not automatically add you to the optional WhatsApp group.

We may contact members by email with information directly related to their membership and Community participation.

Optional promotional marketing communications are managed separately, in accordance with applicable law.

Community participation is subject to the CBA Community Guidelines & Terms of Participation.

You may leave the Community at any time by contacting us or leaving the relevant communication channels.

8. Instagram, WhatsApp and Other Community Platforms

Our main Community Chat operates on Instagram. We also offer an optional WhatsApp group for additional discussions and sharing resources.

Participation in these platforms is subject to their respective terms of service and privacy policies.

Depending on the platform and its settings, other members may be able to see your profile information, username, telephone number, messages and other information you choose to share.

We use your Instagram handle to add you to the main Community Chat. You can choose whether to join the optional WhatsApp group.

We encourage members to review the privacy settings of any third-party platforms they use and to consider carefully what personal information they share.

We cannot guarantee the deletion of messages or information already viewed, copied or retained by other participants.

Our website may use technical features necessary for its operation, security and the processing of enquiries and registrations.

15. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or subsequently lawfully used, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

Our retention arrangements include:

  • Consultation enquiries and business contacts: We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage enquiries, maintain relevant professional relationships, follow up on potential opportunities and meet applicable legal or business requirements. We periodically review inactive records and delete or anonymise information that is no longer needed.
  • CBA Community membership information: We retain membership information for the duration of membership, including periods when members are not actively participating. When membership ends, we review what information remains necessary for legitimate administrative, legal or ongoing relationship-management purposes. Information that is no longer needed will be deleted or anonymised.
  • Periodic reviews: We review the personal information we hold at appropriate intervals, generally at least annually, to assess its accuracy, relevance and continued necessity.
  • Event registrations and participation records: For the period reasonably necessary for event administration, follow-up and applicable legal requirements.
  • Client records and correspondence: For the duration of the client relationship and any additional period required for legal, contractual or legitimate business purposes.
  • Marketing information: Until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, subject to periodic review. Limited suppression records may be retained to respect your preferences.
  • Website analytics and technical information: In accordance with the applicable retention settings of the services used, where relevant.
  • Personal information processed on behalf of clients: In accordance with the relevant client agreement and documented instructions, subject to applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymise it.

16. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration and disclosure.

These measures may include access restrictions, account security controls, secure storage and the use of reputable service providers.

Access to personal information is limited to those who require it for legitimate business purposes.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
